### Overload Acceleration
Enchanting the PureMash Core Block with **Overload** unlocks tick acceleration:
* **Overload I (3x3x3 Area):** Accelerates crops and adjacent block entities.
* **Overload II (5x5x5 Area):** Multiplied tick speed.
* **Overload III (7x7x7 Area):** Massive multi-fold tick acceleration across all machines and crops.

* **Controls:** Right-click the block to toggle acceleration (On/Off). **Shift + Right-Click** to toggle the 3D bounding box overlay.
