# Machine Upgrades

PureMash machines feature dedicated upgrade slots to increase speed, multiply energy capacity, duplicate outputs, and process entire stacks at once.

---

## 1. Speed Upgrades

<ItemGrid>
  <ItemIcon id="puremashtweaks:speed_upgrade_1" />
  <ItemIcon id="puremashtweaks:speed_upgrade_2" />
  <ItemIcon id="puremashtweaks:speed_upgrade_3" />
</ItemGrid>

* **Speed Tier 1:** Adds +2 operational speed per card.
* **Speed Tier 2:** Adds +4 operational speed per card.
* **Speed Tier 3:** Multiplies speed exponentially (+16, +64, and up to +256 speed when fully stacked with 3x Tier 3 cards).

## 2. Capacity & Duplication

<ItemGrid>
  <ItemIcon id="puremashtweaks:capacity_upgrade_1" />

  <ItemIcon id="puremashtweaks:duplication_upgrade_1" />

* **Capacity Tier 1 (2x) & Tier 2 (5x):** Multiplies internal FE capacity across machines.
* **Duplication Tier 1 (15%) & Tier 2 (50%):** Grants a chance to produce bonus duplicate outputs per completed recipe cycle in compatible modes.
