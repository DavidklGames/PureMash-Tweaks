# Credits & Acknowledgments

**PureMash Tweaks** was created as a gameplay enhancement and integration mod. Several of our mechanics draw inspiration from classic community projects.

---

## 1. Core Inspirations

<Row gap="10">
  <Column fullWidth={true}>
    ### [<Color id="puremashtweaks:cyan_glow">Pipez (by Henkelmax)</Color>]
    Our **Universal Cables**—combining Items, Fluids, and Energy into a single conduit with configurable extraction and filtering—were inspired by the practical logistics design of *Pipez*.
  </Column>
</Row>

<Row gap="10">
  <Column fullWidth={true}>
    ### [<Color id="puremashtweaks:cyan_glow">Avaritia & Re:Avaritia</Color>]
    The material compression progression, gravitational singularities, and the **Cosmic Singularity** pay homage to *Avaritia*.
    * **Halo Visuals:** The iconic Halo texture belongs to *Avaritia*, adapted to work within the Minecraft **26.1.2** rendering pipeline.
  </Column>
</Row>

<Row gap="10">
  <Column fullWidth={true}>
    ### [<Color id="puremashtweaks:cyan_glow">Applied Energistics 2 & GuideME</Color>]
    The automation safety of the **Synthesis Table** and Memory Card patterns draw inspiration from *Applied Energistics 2*. This in-game guidebook is powered by *GuideME*.
  </Column>
</Row>

---

## 2. Compatibility & Extras

PureMash Tweaks includes native recipe and feature support for several community mods:

* **Productive Bees & Productive Metalworks (by LobsterJonn):** Custom bees (SynthBee/MoldelBee) and high-temperature foundry casting.
* **Silent Gear (by SilentChaos512):** Custom material traits and tool rod part substitutions.
* **KubeJS (by LatvianModder):** Custom recipe schema integration for pack creators.

---

## 3. Original Audio

* **Music Discs:** The tracks *Beyond The Final Stage* and *New Horizons*, along with the goal completion fanfare (the sound that plays when all achievements are unlocked), are original audio compositions by DavidklGames created for the mod.
