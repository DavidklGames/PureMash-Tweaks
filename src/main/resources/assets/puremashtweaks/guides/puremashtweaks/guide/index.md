# PureMash Tweaks

Welcome to the official documentation for **PureMash Tweaks**.

This mod adds high-tier metal alloys, expansive 9x9 automated crafting, multi-mode compression machinery, thermodynamic power generation, 3-in-1 universal logistics, and creative-grade endgame components.

---

## Progression Overview

Getting started with PureMash Tweaks involves a clear path of resource gathering and machine expansion:

1. **Prospecting:** Mine in the deepslate layer between **Y = -64** and **Y = 0** in the Overworld to find <ItemLink id="puremashtweaks:synthorium_debris" />.
2. **First Ingot:** Smelt the debris to get <ItemLink id="puremashtweaks:synthorium_scrap" />, then combine 4 scraps with 4 diamonds to craft your first <ItemLink id="puremashtweaks:synthorium_ingot" />.
3. **The Paxel:** Forge a <ItemLink id="puremashtweaks:synthorium_paxel" /> to cover all mining needs in a single durable tool.
4. **Early Machinery:** Build the <ItemLink id="puremashtweaks:multifunctional_compressor" /> and the <ItemLink id="puremashtweaks:synthesis_table" /> to start processing high-density recipes and singularities.
5. **The Void & Transcendent Metals:** Head to the End to locate the Moldelonian Smithing Template, forge <ItemLink id="puremashtweaks:moldelonian_ingot" />, condense the Cosmic Singularity, and assemble the <ItemLink id="puremashtweaks:puremash_core" />.

## Keybinds & Controls

* **GuideME Quick Lookup:** Hover over any mod item in your inventory or JEI and press **G** to jump straight to its guide page.
* **Overload Flight:** Press <KeyBind id="key.puremashtweaks.toggle_flight" /> to toggle creative flight when wearing a full set of Overload-enchanted armor.
* **Overdrive Mode:** Press <KeyBind id="key.puremashtweaks.toggle_overdrive" /> to switch area mining and mobility perks on or off.
* **Clearing Memory Cards:** Hold an encoded Memory Card, look straight up (pitch &lt; -60°), and right-click to wipe stored recipe data.
* **Universal Cables:** **Shift + Right-Click** cable faces with a Configuration Wrench to switch between Insert, Extract, and Disconnect modes.
